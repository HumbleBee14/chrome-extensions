◒Intentional

Your attention. Your data.

Intentional helps you manage time on websites and specific paths. It has no account, server, analytics, ads, or remote code. All extension data stays in this Chrome profile on your device.

What is stored

  • The domains and paths you explicitly monitor, their limits, and prompt settings, saved reason choices, and message colors.
  • Daily usage totals by rule and combined active-time totals for Day and Week views, retained for 30 local calendar days.
  • The reasons you enter, visit dates, matching rule names, and selected session durations. Logs are retained for 30 days, up to 1,000 entries and 2 MiB. The oldest entries are removed first.
  • Session and override deadlines and reasons, daily locks, numbered override attempts, and time spent over your limit. Counters and logs are retained for 30 days. Temporary tab identifiers support active-tab tracking.

We don’t store page content or a browsing history of unmonitored sites. URL queries and fragments aren’t included in intention logs. Reasons are personal: avoid including passwords or other secrets.

Records use Chrome’s local and session extension storage, restricted to trusted extension contexts. Intentional does not add its own encryption layer; local access protection depends on your Chrome profile and device.

Website permissions

When you save a rule, Chrome asks for access to that website. Access covers the host, because Chrome grants permissions by host rather than by individual path. Intentional checks the paths you select, displays prompts and warnings, pauses page media while gated, and redirects locked navigations. You can revoke access in Chrome’s extension settings.

Optional extension-page guard

The guard is off by default. Enabling it requests the optional tabs permission to recognize Chrome’s extension-manager URLs and redirect them to a reminder. Tab URLs are inspected in memory and are not logged. This is a best-effort friction feature; it does not prevent disabling or uninstalling through other routes. You can turn it off directly on the reminder or in Intentional settings. Strong uninstall protection requires administrator-managed Chrome policies outside this extension.

Control your records

The Activity log lets you export your records, clear a single day, or clear the selected day or week. Clearing logs doesn’t reset your usage or unlock pages. “Erase all data” removes rules, usage, logs, timers, locks, override counts, and over-limit totals. Uninstalling also removes extension storage. Exported files are yours to manage and delete.

Limited use

Information accessed through Chrome APIs is used only to provide the disclosed website and path time-limit features. It is never sold, transmitted to third parties, used for advertising, or used for unrelated purposes. This use adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Practical boundaries

Daily time counts in the active tab while Chrome is focused, and pauses when the screen is locked. Reading and watching in the focused tab still count. Session timers measure elapsed time, even in other tabs or after a browser restart. Locks reset at local midnight. Intentional is a voluntary focus tool: settings remain editable, and it cannot prevent you from disabling it. Incognito, Chrome internal pages, local files, and embedded frames aren’t supported.
